##### Encryption key 

A three-word string, generated by your Flex every 30 seconds. Access the encryption key from the Flex touchscreen and enter it into your Opentrons App to establish (or re-establish) the connection between your compliance ready Flex and app.

###### Service PIN

A PIN provided by an Opentrons-trained representative and used to activate Opentrons Flex Compliance Ready Software. Your lab will never need to access this PIN again.
