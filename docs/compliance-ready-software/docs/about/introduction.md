[Opentrons Flex Compliance-Ready software](https://opentrons.com/compliance-ready-software) is installed on your Flex liquid handling robot to enable 21 CFR part 11-ready operation. The software: 

* Captures every user action with timestamps, documentation, and robot-generated log files.
* Enforces role-based access, including different permissions and required logins, for administrators and users.
* Changes how you interact with your Flex across the Opentrons App and Flex touchscreen.

Compliance-Ready software irreversibly adds technical controls, like required documentation and log files, to comply with FDA regulation 21 CFR part 11. After installation by an Opentrons-trained representative, you'll add your own validations, protocols, and data management practices to make your Flex fully audit-ready.
