It's important to be able to verify the audit logs your compliance ready Flex generates. Since these, like other robot files, are difficult for humans to read, you can use the Opentrons Log Verifier App to verify protocol data captured in audit logs and log periods.  

!!! note
    The Opentrons Log Verifier App is separate from the Opentrons App you use to control your Flex. You can download the app for Windows, Mac, or Linux from the Opentrons Github page. 

The audit logs and other files your Flex generates are never viewed or stored by Opentrons. It's your lab's responsibility to manage these files, including downloading and storing in a secure location. The Opentrons Log Verifier App never stores your files, and can only be used for understanding and verifying your audit logs.

## Setup

After you download the Log Verifier App, you'll need to choose the directory containing your audit logs. You can configure the [default storage location](../settings.md#download-folder) in the Opentrons App.

<figure class="screenshot" markdown>
  ![Log verifier before you choose a working directory.](../images/log-verifier.png)
  <figcaption>Click on the left to choose the directory containing your audit logs.</figcaption>
</figure> 

If you need to choose a new directory, click the :material-plus: at the top left.

Remember that your audit logs are grouped together and downloaded in a *log period*. Log periods end after the Flex completes each protocol run or whenever the Flex boots, like after a robot software update.

You can download one or more log periods as a `.zip` file from the Opentrons App or Flex touchscreen. To view them in the Log Verifier App, first expand the `.zip` file into a folder. Then, choose the folder as your directory to view all the log periods in the Log Verifier App. 

When you look at a robot's audit logs for the first time, you'll also need to  authorize that robot's identity. The log verifier will prompt you to review your robot's name, serial number, and certificate. Click **Trust this robot** to continue. 

## View a log period 

Click to expand the **Devices** list on the left and choose your robot. Then, choose a log period. 

<figure class="screenshot" markdown>
  ![View a log period and its entries in the log verifier.](../images/log-period-verifier.png)
  <figcaption>View a log period and its entries in the log verifier.</figcaption>
</figure> 

Each log period contains actions, or log entries, completed during that period. Here, each row shows: 

* A UTC timestamp for the action. 
* A phrase describing the action, like "Start update session" for a software update.
* The username and legal name of the user responsible for the action.
* The action's status (pass or fail). 

Every log entry within a period is labeled with a "pass" in green or a "fail" in red. When every entry in your log period has all the data it should from your protocol, it passes and is verified. 

A failing log entry means that every piece of protocol data can't be accounted for in the audit log. In these cases, you should not consider your audit log to be verified.

## View log details

Click any individual log entry to view in its own page. Here, you'll see additional information about the action:

* The **reason** for the action, or the [user's documentation](documentation.md).
* Additional compliance-ready information, like an entry hash and signature.
