## Collars

Collars establish a strong, airtight seal between your filter plate and the rest of the labware stack to help ensure proper vacuum pressure. They also feature custom adapters, making them fully compatible with the Flex Gripper.

The module ships with two collar sizes to accommodate different filter plate volumes:

* **Short Collar (42 mm):** Designed to hold standard ANSI/SLAS compliant filter plates.
* **Tall Collar (72 mm):** Accommodates deep-well filter plates (typically up to 2 mL deep).

## Spacers

Spacers fit internally within the manifold base. They help reduce the gap between the top filter plate and the bottom collection plate.

The module ships with two spacer sizes to accommodate varying collection plate heights:

* **Short Spacer:** 27 mm
* **Tall Spacer:** 34 mm

## Use

### Elution and Filtrate Collection

This configuration is used when a protocol requires collecting the filtered liquid (the eluate or specific waste) into a receiver plate inside the manifold. 

An internal spacer is required for this setup. Spacers properly position the receiver plate directly beneath the sample filter plate, reducing the space between these labware. Keeping a minimal gap between the filter and receiver plates helps ensure that the liquid drops cleanly into the receiver wells rather than being pulled sideways by the vacuum.

### Direct Filter to Waste

This configuration is used when the liquid being pulled through the filter plate does not need to be saved and is meant to be discarded.

An internal spacer or collection plate is not required inside the manifold for this setup. Without a collection plate, the vacuum module pulls waste straight into the manifold base, through the connected hose, and out to the off-deck 2 liter carboy.
