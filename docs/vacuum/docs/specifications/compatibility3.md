## Compatibility key

The chemical compatibility tables use these ratings:

* :material-check-circle:{ .dark-green } **Excellent** — No degradation after continuous exposure.
* :material-check-circle-outline:{ .light-green } **Good** — Occasional minor effects (e.g., swelling or discoloration); suitable for long-term use.
* :material-alert-circle-outline:{ .gray } **Fair / Marginal** — Moderate effects; Use with caution, flush after exposure.
* :material-minus-circle-outline:{ .yellow } **Poor** — Severe degradation; brief or accidental contact only.
* :material-close-circle:{ .red } **Not Recommended** — Rapid breakdown or complete material failure.

## Compatibility summary

This is a high-level overview of general compatibility for Vacuum Module parts that can have direct chemical contact. For specific chemical and parts suitability, see the [reagent index](#reagent-index) below.

| Part | Material | Summary |
|----|----|----|
| **Connectors and carboy float** | Polypropylene | The CPC quick connect/disconnect pieces and the carboy float are resistant to aqueous solutions, biological buffers, alcohols, along with salts and oxidizing washes. They're less resistant to non-polar organic solvents (e.g., Hexane, Toluene), chlorinated solvents (e.g., Methylene Chloride), and concentrated fuming acids (e.g., Nitric Acid, Aqua Regia). |
| **Deck pieces** | Nylon 6 (PA6) | The vacuum base, collars, and spacers are resistant to alkalis, esters, and hydrocarbons. They're less resistant to strong acids (e.g., sulfuric acid, hydrochloric acid) and oxidizing agents. |
| **Gaskets** | Silicone 3003/20 | The gasket material on external deck pieces is resistant to aqueous solutions, dilute acids, bases, and alcohols. It is less resistant to concentrated acids, non-polar organic solvents (e.g., Hexane, Toluene), ketones (e.g., Acetone), and strong hydrocarbons. |
| **Vacuum hose** | Tygon® 2375 | The 6 mm and 9 mm external vacuum hoses are resistant to aqueous solutions, acids, bases, ketones, salts, and alcohols. They're less resistant to aromatic and chlorinated hydrocarbons (e.g., Toluene, Hexane, Methylene Chloride/DCM, Tetrahydrofuran/THF, Ethyl Acetate). |
