Module deck components consist of a vacuum base and two sets of interchangeable collars and spacers. The vacuum base is the foundation of this hardware stack. The collars and spacers control labware stacking height for different types of automated vacuum filtration protocols. Together, these components help ensure a good vacuum seal, whether your material is being saved as an eluate or discarded as waste.

## Vacuum base

The vacuum base sits directly on the deck plate adapter. It is the foundation of the module's hardware stack, supporting the spacers, collars, and protocol labware.

When under vacuum pressure, the base allows fluid to be pulled through the assembly, routing it into a collection well plate or to the attached waste jar via the attached 6 mm hose.

## Collars

Collars sit directly on the manifold base to support filter plates during vacuum extraction protocols. Each collar features an integrated gasket that forms an airtight vacuum seal with the well plate, while a secondary gasket on the manifold base seals it to the collar. The short and tall collars can be used with either spacer and are fully compatible with the Flex Gripper for automated protocol operations.

The Vacuum Module includes two collars:

* **Short Collar (42 mm):** Accommodates standard microliter (μL) filter plates.
* **Tall Collar (72 mm):** Accommodates deep-well filter plates (typically up to 2 mL).

## Spacers

Spacers sit inside the manifold base and hold the internal collection labware at the correct height for filtration.

In a vacuum filtration protocol, spacers minimize the vertical clearance between the source filter plate and the destination collection plate. A small plate gap helps ensure fluid droplets drop cleanly and directly into the receiving wells, preventing liquid from being pulled sideways by vacuum pressure and cross-contaminating other wells. The short and tall spacers can be used with either collar.

The Vacuum Module ships with two spacer sizes to match varying labware profiles:

* **Short Spacer:** 27 mm
* **Tall Spacer:** 34 mm

## Typical Use

### Elution and Filtrate Collection

This configuration is used when a protocol requires capturing the filtered liquid (the eluate or a target filtrate) into a collection plate placed inside the manifold base.

* **Internal Hardware:** Placing the short or tall spacer inside the manifold base helps position filtrate collection labware close to the source filter plate.
* **Fluid Routing:** Reducing the gap between source and collection plates helps ensure that liquid flows vertically through the filter plate and drips straight into the corresponding collection wells.

### Direct Filter to Waste

This configuration is used when the liquid being pulled through the filter plate is unwanted and can be discarded to the waste collection jar.

* **Internal Hardware:** No internal spacer or collection plate is required. The inside of the manifold base remains completely empty. The hardware stack still needs a tall or short collar and a liquid source in a filter plate.
* **Fluid Routing:** Vacuum pressure draws liquid straight through the filter plate into the manifold base, pulling it through the connected vacuum hose and to the off-deck waste collection jar.
