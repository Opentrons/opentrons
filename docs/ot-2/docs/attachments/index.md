title: "Opentrons OT-2: Instruments and Modules"
---

The OT-2 is a remarkable robot by itself, but it needs specialized gantry and deck mounted components to manipulate liquids and automate the work done in a typical lab. Instruments and modules are the two classes of hardware that the OT-2 uses to perform all its tasks during protocol execution.

- [Instruments](./instruments.md) are the single- and multi-channel pipettes that attach to the gantry and move liquids throughout the working area during protocol execution.

- [Modules](./modules/index.md) are hardware deck-mounted hardware modules that add features and capabilities to the robot. The OT-2 communicates and controls most modules through a USB connection.

This chapter summarizes the functions and physical specifications of Opentrons modules that are compatible with an OT-2.

## Supported modules

- Pipettes
- Attaching
- Calibration




## Modules
