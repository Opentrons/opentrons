---
title: "Opentrons OT-2: Installation and Relocation"
---

This chapter describes how to prepare your lab for an OT-2, how to set up the robot, and how to move it if necessary.

- Before taking delivery of your OT-2, make sure your lab or facility meets the all the criteria in the [Installation Requirements](requirements.md) section.

- When it's time to get your OT-2 up and running, follow the instructions in the [Unboxing](unboxing.md), [First Run](first-run.md), and [Instruments and Calibration](instruments.md) section.

- If you ever need to move your OT-2 to a new location, near or far, refer to the [Relocation](relocation.md) section.
