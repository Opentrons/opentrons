After initial robot setup, the next step is to attach instruments to the robot and calibrate them. All instruments have an automated calibration procedure, which you should perform immediately after installation.

## OT-2 Pipette mounts

The OT-2 has two pipette mounts. Each pipette mount can hold a single 1-channel and/or 8-channel pipette. Additionally, the pipette installation process varies depending on if you're attaching a GEN1 or GEN2 pipette. So, you must first determine your pipette type before you attach it. The information in this section will help you identify a GEN1 or GEN2 pipette and install it on your robot.
