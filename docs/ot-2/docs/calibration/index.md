title: "Opentrons OT-2: Calibration"
description: "Deck, tip length, pipette offset, and labware calibration procedures."
---

This chapter covers the calibration procedures and software tools you will use to ensure your OT-2 moves around its working area with repeatable accuracy. By mapping the robot's hardware and labware to the deck and gantry-mounted instruments, these procedures help the robot operate with precision during a protocol run.

- Build and maintain the foundation of your robot's positional accuracy by performing the deck, tip length, and pipette offset calibrations described in the [Robot Calibration section](./robot-calibration.md).

- To further improve your robot's understanding of the the physical dimensions and deck locations of protocol labware compared with their software definitions, follow the procedures in the [Labware Calibration section](./labware-offsets.md).

- Whenever you need to make fine adjustments along the x-, y-, and z-axes during these alignment procedures, see the [Jog Controls section](./jog-controls.md).
